# 010 — Terminology and Glossary

## Purpose
This file fixes vocabulary so later technical documents do not drift into different machine architectures.

## THERMA
GHIA's proposed reversible thermoacoustic thermal platform. The term covers the system-level concept, not a claim of validated hardware performance.

## TA-CRX
The historical internal engineering codename used during the project. It should be treated as project history rather than the current product-family name.

## Thermoacoustic engine
A device that converts an imposed thermal gradient into acoustic work through oscillating gas dynamics and gas-solid thermal interaction.

## Thermoacoustic refrigerator
A thermoacoustic device that uses acoustic work to remove heat from a cold side and reject it to a warmer side.

## Thermoacoustic heat pump
A thermoacoustic device that uses work to move heat from a lower-temperature source to a higher-temperature sink.

## Working gas
The pressurized gas participating in the acoustic thermodynamic cycle. Helium is a recurring candidate in the project, but final selection remains open.

## Acoustic core
The fixed set of resonator, regenerator, heat exchangers and related impedance elements that create and exploit the thermoacoustic field.

## Resonator
A fixed acoustic volume/path that establishes pressure and velocity fields and their phase relationships.

## Inertance
An acoustic element that stores kinetic energy associated with gas motion and contributes reactive impedance.

## Compliance
An acoustic volume that stores potential energy associated with gas compression/expansion.

## Acoustic impedance
The relationship between acoustic pressure and volume velocity at a defined point or port, including resistive and reactive components.

## Regenerator / stack
A porous or structured solid matrix that exchanges heat with the oscillating gas and participates in the thermoacoustic cycle.

## Thermal penetration depth
A characteristic diffusion length describing how far oscillatory thermal disturbances penetrate from the gas into the near-wall region during one cycle.

## Viscous boundary layer
The near-wall region where oscillatory viscous effects strongly reduce gas velocity and create losses.

## Gedeon streaming
Steady mean mass transport in looped thermoacoustic systems that can carry heat parasitically.

## Acoustic streaming
Mean fluid motion generated by nonlinear acoustic effects. It can increase losses and alter heat transport.

## Heat exchanger approach
The temperature difference between a fluid stream and the thermal node it is exchanging with. Smaller approaches generally require more surface area and better heat-transfer performance.

## Secondary loop
An external thermal-fluid circuit that transports useful heating or cooling without exposing the user loop to the pressurized acoustic gas.

## Heat sink
The environment or thermal reservoir accepting rejected heat.

## Heat source
The external resource supplying thermal energy or, in heat-pump mode, the low-temperature reservoir from which heat is extracted.

## Cooling capacity Qc
The rate of heat removal from the cold side, normally stated in watts or kilowatts.

## Heating capacity Qh
The rate of useful heat delivery to the hot side.

## Thermal-input COP
Cooling or heating output divided by supplied thermal driving energy when the denominator is defined as heat input. The convention must always be stated.

## Work-input COP
Cooling or heating output divided by externally supplied work such as acoustic/electrical work.

## Neutral state
A controlled reversible-operation state in which the active thermal/acoustic routing is stabilized before changing mode.

## ATAPR
A project label for adaptive thermoacoustic/thermal routing and control. It is not an established external standard term.

## Prototype-ready
A design state sufficiently specified for controlled physical development; it does not mean production certified.

## Production-ready
A materially different state requiring validated engineering, safety qualification, conformity/certification as applicable, manufacturing controls and repeatable performance.

## Design-study dimension
A provisional dimension used for analysis or illustration. It is not an approved fabrication dimension unless independently derived and released.

## Screening simulation
A simplified computational model used to explore sensitivity, compare architectures or identify promising operating regions.

## High-fidelity model
A validated or independently benchmarked model representing geometry and physics with sufficient fidelity for its intended prediction task.

## Hardware correlation
Comparison between measured prototype data and predicted model outputs under matched boundary conditions.

## Architecture drift
An unintended change in the machine concept during documentation or illustration. The piston/Stirling-like images generated late in the first visual pass are examples of drift and are not THERMA 3.5 architecture.

## 3.3
The project generation focused on rapid 0–3 °C pull-down using modular/parallel capacity and thermal buffering concepts.

## 3.4
The project generation focused on regenerator and heat-exchanger optimization, streaming mitigation, modularity and favorable 80–120 °C industrial operation.

## 3.5
The current reversible platform concept that combines the fixed thermoacoustic core with cooling and heating modes.
