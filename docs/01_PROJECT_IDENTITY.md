# 01 — Project Identity and Scope

## Purpose
Define what GHIA THERMA is, where it sits inside GHIA, and what this repository is intended to document.

## GHIA relationship
THERMA is a GHIA project and product concept. The repository documents the thermoacoustic thermal platform rather than a legally registered company entity.

## Product family
The product-family name is **THERMA**. The earlier internal engineering codename was **TA-CRX**. TA-CRX may remain in historical records when it is useful for tracing design evolution, but THERMA 3.5 is the current architecture name.

## Technical scope
THERMA is a proposed reversible thermoacoustic thermal platform. The intended functions are:

1. heat-driven thermoacoustic refrigeration;
2. reversible thermoacoustic heat-pump heating;
3. controlled interfaces to waste heat and low-temperature sources;
4. secondary water/thermal-fluid delivery to user loads;
5. modular fixed acoustic cores;
6. adaptive control and safety isolation;
7. serviceable architecture rather than an assumption of maintenance-free operation.

## Architecture constraint
The current baseline is a **no-mechanical-moving-parts thermoacoustic core**. Gas oscillation is fluid/acoustic motion, not a mechanical piston train. The baseline therefore excludes piston, displacer, crankshaft, connecting rod, flywheel, and mechanical bearing assemblies from the thermoacoustic core.

## Evidence status
The project is conceptual/pre-prototype. Existing thermoacoustic science is established, but THERMA-specific performance, exact geometry, material life, pressure integrity, certification, economics, and commercial readiness remain to be validated.

## Primary questions
- Can one exact fixed geometry achieve useful acoustic power density?
- Can it deliver measurable 0–3 °C refrigeration under a favorable heat source?
- Can the reversible mode provide useful hot-water heating?
- Can heat exchangers and regenerators remain practical under oscillatory flow?
- Can the system be competitive where waste heat is already available?

## Repository rule
Each subsequent document should identify whether its claims are established, calculated, model-based, or hardware-dependent.
